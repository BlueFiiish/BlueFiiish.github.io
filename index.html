Our story

A home-grown bakery, six years in the making

Ana baked from home for six years before she found the right spot on Third Street in Historic Downtown Rosenberg. She fell in love with the space and opened the doors in March 2020.

Now neighbors stop in for a custom cake one day and a coffee and a brownie the next. Both are made fresh on Third Street.
