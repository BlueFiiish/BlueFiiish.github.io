Our story

Meet Ana

A home-grown bakery

Before there was a storefront, there was a home kitchen. Ana baked from home for six years, building a following one custom cake and cookie order at a time.

In March 2020, she found the right spot on Third Street in Historic Downtown Rosenberg. She instantly fell in love with the space and knew it was the perfect home for her bakery.

These days it's where neighbors pick up a celebration cake one week and a coffee and a brownie the next. Come say hi. Ana's usually in the kitchen.

Made to order

Every custom cake and cookie is designed and baked for your celebration.

Baked fresh

Cupcakes, brownies, cookies, muffins and coffee, fresh in-store Wed–Sun.
