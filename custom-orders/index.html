FAQ

Common questions

How do I place an order?

The quickest way is Ana's online order form (opens in a new tab), which captures your date, design photos and contact details. You can also call (979) 533-0651, or send a quick question using the form below.

How far in advance should I order?

Order as early as you can, especially for weddings. The sooner you reach out, the more likely Ana can fit your date.

Do you do weddings, groom's cakes and custom cookies?

Yes. Ana makes wedding cakes, groom's cakes and custom decorated cookies, along with birthday and kids' party cakes. You can see examples on the gallery.
